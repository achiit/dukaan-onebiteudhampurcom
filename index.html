Rutik, owner

In 2024, I opened OneBite in Shiv Nagar with a small kitchen and a big idea: good fast food that does not hurt your pocket. My family thought I was crazy to start with burgers at Rs. 49, but that is how we got our first regulars.

Now students, office staff and whole families come in, and the bill is usually somewhere between Rs. 200 and Rs. 800. We make the sauces ourselves each morning, and I am at the counter every day.

Come in, say hello, and tell us what you want less or more of. That is how our menu was built.

  • Cooked fresh after you order, never reheated
  • Sauces made in our own kitchen every morning
  • Clean open kitchen, you can watch your food being made
  • Hot food packed in sealed boxes for delivery
  • Owner is at the counter every evening
